By studying the geochemistry, hydrology, methane, microbes, and ecosystems, our team has led to multiple microbial discoveries and provided evidence-based mechanistic understanding of CH4 in Amazon peatlands, a novel and critical ecosystem in the tropics. Eddy covariance has emerged as an approach accessible to remote locations, capable to integrate global monitoring frameworks, and to provide tangible benefits to local-communities, hence our efforts proposes an Amazon Peatlands for Climate Solutions Inititaive. In this seminar we provide an overview on mapping carbon, flux records and proposes a community-based mechanism to steward soil carbon and Eddie Covariance records in the Amazon rainforest
