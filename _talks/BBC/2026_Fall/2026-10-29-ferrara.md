Florida manatees are among one of the state's most recognizable and beloved marine mammals. However, they face numerous natural and human-related challenges. I will discuss how to recognize signs of distress or injury in manatees, identify abnormal behaviors, and understand when an animal may need intervention. The presentation will also offer an inside look at the role of an FWC Marine Mammal Biologist, from assessing reported animals to coordinating rescue operations.
